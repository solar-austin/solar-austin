About this calculator

This version is intentionally simple. It simulates one year of monthly and hourly load versus solar output, values imports at your retail rate, values exports at your buyback rate, uses a 4-hour battery that charges from solar surplus and discharges into later deficits, and rolls the savings forward for 30 years using your selected utility escalation assumption.

Austin Energy calculation details

Austin Energy mode uses the published inside-city residential electric tariff and the current Value of Solar rate. The tool bills all monthly usage using Austin Energy's tiered energy charge, customer charge, power supply adjustment, PSA administrative adjustment, regulatory charge, community benefit charge, and 1% city sales tax.

Solar is modeled under Austin Energy's Value of Solar structure: all home consumption is billed normally, all solar generation is credited at 9.91 cents/kWh, and excess electric credit carries forward month to month.

This mode assumes an inside-city residential account and keeps rates fixed at the currently published schedule rather than projecting future tariff changes.

  • Customer charge: $16.50/month
  • Tier 1 energy (0-300 kWh): 4.640 cents/kWh
  • Tier 2 energy (301-900 kWh): 5.138 cents/kWh
  • Tier 3 energy (901-2,000 kWh): 7.525 cents/kWh
  • Tier 4 energy (>2,000 kWh): 10.884 cents/kWh
  • Power supply adjustment: 4.118 cents/kWh
  • PSA administrative adjustment: -0.206 cents/kWh
  • Regulatory charge: 1.338 cents/kWh
  • Community benefit charge: 1.275 cents/kWh
  • City sales tax: 1.0%

Sources: Residential Rates, Value of Solar Rate, City of Austin Utilities rates PDF.

Austin Energy Rebates

  • Solar rebate: ~$2,500 flat for qualifying installs (subject to availability — pre-approval required)
  • Battery rebate: ~$2,500 per battery (same availability conditions)
