How this calculator works

This version is intentionally simple. It simulates one year of monthly and hourly load versus solar output, values imports at your retail rate, values exports at your buyback rate, uses a 4-hour battery that charges from solar surplus and discharges into later deficits, and rolls the savings forward for 20 years using your selected utility escalation and solar degradation assumptions.
